**New: motion smoothing**
- AnimeJaNai's RIFE frame interpolation (model v4.26) for anime: 24 fps becomes your display's refresh rate, up to 60 fps (23.976 → 59.94 on a 60 Hz screen). It runs in the same chain as the AI upscale, together with RTX Video HDR.
- Off by default. Turn it on in **Settings → Player → Motion Smoothing (RIFE)**, from the controller's player menu (Y), or with **Ctrl+M**. A choice made while watching becomes the default.
- The model is included (11 MB); nothing extra to download. On an RTX 5070 Ti, 1080p anime smoothed to 60 fps and upscaled to 4K plays without dropped frames at about 72% GPU.

StremioZ 0.9.x installs update to this release on their own (Settings → StremioZ → Check now, or at the next start). New installs: run `StremioZ-Setup-0.9.2.exe`.
