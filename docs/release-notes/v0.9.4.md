**New**
- **Skip intro from three sources, in this order:**
  1. the video's own chapters named Intro, OP, Ending, ED and so on;
  2. AniSkip for anime, else IntroDB for other shows;
  3. silence detection (press skip twice to fast-forward to where the audio goes quiet).

  Skip is Tab, or X on the controller. IntroDB never worked in earlier StremioZ versions; it does now.
- **Fullscreen button in the player's control bar**, at the right end, so the controller can reach it.

**Changed**
- **Snappier controller navigation.** Moving the highlight scrolls the page almost at once, and the interface's hover animations are much shorter in TV mode.
- **The right stick scrolls smoothly while held**, and the highlight moves with the list instead of waiting for the scroll to stop.
- **Opening a show jumps straight to your next episode** instead of slowly scrolling to it.
- **The StremioZ icon** replaces the Stremio Kai logo at the top left.
- **The Anime banner works while Jikan is down:** it takes the day's airing anime from AniList instead.

**Fixed**
- Leaving fullscreen with the mouse could take several clicks after the controller had made the window fullscreen. Fullscreen is now one state for the mouse, keyboard and controller.

StremioZ 0.9.x installs update to this release on their own (Settings → StremioZ → Check now, or at the next start). New installs: run `StremioZ-Setup-0.9.4.exe`.
