**New**
- **Three home banner modes: Movies, Anime and For You.** Stremio Kai's Movies and Anime banners are back next to For You. The arrows button at the top right switches between them, and the banner remembers your choice. For You stays the default, and if you were seeing it, you keep seeing it with your current picks.
- **Motion smoothing button in the player**, left of the speed button. It lights up while motion smoothing (RIFE) runs and is dimmed when it's on but the video doesn't get it (it works on anime upscaled by AnimeJaNai). Same as Ctrl+M.
- **Remembered playback speed.** Videos start at the speed you last chose instead of 1×. To turn this off, go to Settings → StremioZ → Playback.

**Changed**
- Settings → StremioZ → For You → Refresh now clears only the For You picks; the Movies and Anime banners keep theirs.

The Anime banner takes its titles from the Jikan anime schedule service. While Jikan is unreachable, it shows placeholder titles and tries again the next time you switch to it.

StremioZ 0.9.x installs update to this release on their own (Settings → StremioZ → Check now, or at the next start). New installs: run `StremioZ-Setup-0.9.3.exe`.
